Hexavera maintains strict data governance standards. Your workforce data remains under your control with transparent processing practices.

## Data governance commitment

Enterprise workforce data requires enterprise-grade protection. Hexavera processes data exclusively for workforce stability outcomes defined in your service agreement.

### How data is governed

**1. Data Collection**

Hexavera collects workforce scheduling, attendance, and compliance data as defined in your enterprise agreement. No personal data is processed beyond operational requirements.

**2. Data Processing**

All data processing occurs within your designated region. Predictive models operate on aggregated patterns, not individual records. Data remains within your organizational boundary.

**3. Data Retention**

Retention periods align with your compliance requirements and regional regulations. Data deletion occurs within 30 days of contract termination upon request.

**4. Third-Party Access**

No workforce data is shared with third parties for marketing or advertising. Infrastructure providers process data only under data processing agreements.

**5. Your Rights**

Enterprise administrators maintain full control over data access, export, and deletion. Personal data is processed under the GDPR and the privacy law that applies to each deployment, as your data processing agreement sets out.
