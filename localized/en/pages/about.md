  - type: section
    eyebrow: "Why decisions need a record"
    title: "Plants keep records of people and pay, but rarely of the decisions in between"
    markdown: |
      An industrial plant decides about its people all day. Who may enter the high-care area. Who covers the night shift. Whether a correction to a timecard stands. Whether overtime was approved. Each decision rests on a rule, a value and a few facts.

      The HR system records the person. Payroll records the payment. The decision in between usually lives in a spreadsheet, an email or a supervisor's memory. When a works council, an auditor or a labour inspector asks why something happened, the answer has to be reconstructed.

      We think the decision deserves its own record: made against a written rule, enforced where it happens, stored with its evidence and explained in plain language. That is what Hexavera is for.
