  - type: faq
    id: "faq"
    title: "Questions about the first conversation"
    items:
      - question: "What is the first conversation about?"
        answer: |
          Your plant, not our slides. We ask how many sites and people you have, which systems hold your people, time and access data, and which working-time rules and agreements you work under. You leave with a clear view of what connects today and what is on the plan.
      - question: "Who should join from our side?"
        answer: |
          Whoever owns the decision you want to explain. Usually that is HR or operations, with IT joining when we reach systems and security. Procurement and legal join later, when there is something to contract.
      - question: "What is the compliance diagnostic?"
        answer: |
          We import a period of your own time and access history, run the working-time rules over it, and report each finding with the explanation behind it: the rule, the records it read and what it computed. See [the diagnostic](/diagnostic).
      - question: "What data do you need for the diagnostic, and how is it protected?"
        answer: |
          Time records, shifts and the people they belong to, as files. Personal data is accepted only under a data processing agreement, signed before the first file is sent. You decide what is included.
      - question: "Do we have to replace our current systems?"
        answer: |
          No. Your HR, payroll, ERP and access systems stay. Hexavera connects to them and keeps the record of the decisions made from their data. See [integrations](/integrations).
      - question: "Can we see the product before we talk?"
        answer: |
          Yes. The [product tour](/demo) is an interactive tour with sample data.
