## Enterprise service agreement

These terms govern access to Hexavera's workforce stability platform. Enterprise customers receive customized agreements aligned with organizational requirements.

### 1. Service Scope

Hexavera provides workforce scheduling, attendance, compliance, and predictive analytics services as defined in your enterprise agreement. Service availability targets and maintenance windows are defined in your enterprise agreement.

### 2. Data Ownership

Your organization retains full ownership of all workforce data processed through Hexavera. Platform-generated insights and reports remain your intellectual property.

### 3. Service Levels

Enterprise agreements include defined service levels for platform availability, support response times, and issue resolution. Service credits apply for documented SLA breaches.

### 4. Security Obligations

Hexavera implements the security controls described on its [Security page](/security). Further security obligations are defined in your enterprise agreement.

### 5. Integration Requirements

API access and integration capabilities are governed by technical specifications in your service agreement. Rate limits and data transfer protocols are defined per deployment.

### 6. Term and Termination

Agreement terms are defined in your enterprise contract. Data export and transition support are provided during any contract termination period.

### 7. Limitation of Liability

Liability limitations are defined in your enterprise agreement. Standard limitations apply to indirect, consequential, and punitive damages.

### 8. Governing Law

Enterprise agreements specify applicable jurisdiction. Standard terms are governed by the laws of the Netherlands unless otherwise specified.
